YT Uploader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

What this application is

YT Uploader is a private tool used solely by the owner of a YouTube channel to upload and publish the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the channel owner uses it.

What data it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API v3, and only with the channel owner's own authorization, the application accesses the owner's own YouTube channel for two purposes only: (1) uploading and publishing the owner's own videos, and (2) reading the upload and processing status of those videos. It does not access any other person's channel, videos, or account information.

How credentials are stored

The OAuth credentials used by the application are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server.

No collection, sale, or sharing of data

The application does not collect personal data from any user, does not sell any data, and does not share any data with third parties. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Revoking access

The channel owner may revoke the application's access at any time from the "Third-party apps with account access" section of their Google Account settings. Revoking access immediately stops the application from using the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
